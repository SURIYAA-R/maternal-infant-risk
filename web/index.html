CLINICAL BIOMARKER

Hemoglobin Distribution by Risk Status

Sharp risk escalation when maternal hemoglobin drops below the 11.0 g/dL threshold (anemia). Severe anemia (<7.0 g/dL) correlates with a 4.82x increase in emergency mortality likelihood.

HEMODYNAMICS

Systolic vs Diastolic BP Correlation

Gestational hypertension clustering in high-risk pregnancies (SBP ≥ 140 or DBP ≥ 90 mmHg). Severe pre-eclamptic clustering is strongly segregated along the upper-right quadrant.

PERINATAL OUTCOME

Neonatal Birth Weight Distribution

Severe correlation between infant low birth weight (< 2.5 kg) and adverse mortality prognosis. Median birth weight in the high-risk cohort drops to 1.95 kg vs 3.20 kg in normal deliveries.

OBSTETRIC SEVERITY

Pregnancy Complication Severity

Direct impact of pre-existing complications (pre-eclampsia, placental insufficiency, gestational diabetes) driving over 78% of all critical triage warnings in the population.

DATA ENGINEERING

Class Distribution & SMOTE Balancing

Original dataset class imbalance highlighting the necessity of SMOTE oversampling. Mitigates clinical classifier bias to prevent false negative under-detection of critical patients.

🔍 Expand Visual
EPIDEMIOLOGY

Maternal Age vs Risk (U-Shaped Curve)

Classic U-shaped obstetric risk curve: elevated mortality in teenage mothers (< 18 yrs) due to pelvic immaturity and advanced maternal age (> 35 yrs) due to vascular comorbidities.

🔍 Expand Visual
PREVENTIVE CARE

Antenatal Care (ANC) Protective Effect

Mothers completing ≥ 4 ANC checkups experience drastically lower risk of adverse outcomes (71.8% risk reduction), confirming WHO antenatal surveillance protocols.

🔍 Expand Visual
SOCIOECONOMIC

Urban/Rural & Education Stratification

Socioeconomic gradients: secondary/higher education and urban healthcare access strongly correlate with institutional delivery adherence and lower intrapartum mortality.

🔍 Expand Visual
MULTIVARIATE

Multivariable Correlation Heatmap

Pairwise Pearson correlations across all 12 clinical and socioeconomic predictors, identifying colinear features and guiding tree-based ensemble feature selection.

🔍 Expand Visual
UNSUPERVISED MINING

K-Means Clustering (Elbow & Silhouette)

Unsupervised segmentation discovering natural risk clusters across the 5,000 patient cohort. Optimal k=3 identifies Low, Moderate, and Critical emergency phenotypes.
